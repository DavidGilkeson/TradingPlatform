# Project Atlas — Sprint 31.9 Intelligence Health

Sprint 31 closes with a consolidated Intelligence Health dashboard.

The dashboard measures the maturity and data quality of Atlas intelligence:

- completed paper-trade sample size
- calibration coverage
- exact entry-link coverage
- evidence-qualified setup count
- score/return correlation
- strongest evidence-qualified setup
- strongest evidence-qualified market regime
- Historical Match performance bands

The 0–100 Intelligence Health score deliberately measures evidence maturity,
not profitability. It combines sample depth, calibration coverage, exact
linkage quality and the number of setups that have reached the evidence
threshold.

Paper Trading now has a dedicated Intelligence section containing Health,
Performance, Patterns, Regimes, Setups, Calibration and Journal views.

Sprint 31 is complete: Atlas now records decisions, preserves entry-time
intelligence, links outcomes to exact entries, and measures the quality of the
resulting evidence.
