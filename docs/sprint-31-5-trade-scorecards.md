# Project Atlas — Sprint 31.5 Evidence-Based Trade Scorecards

Atlas can now compare a proposed paper trade with similar completed trades.

The scorecard can match on:

- Atlas Score band
- confidence band
- trend regime
- volatility regime
- verdict / trade reason

It reports:

- Historical Match score (0–100)
- similar completed trades
- win rate
- expectancy per trade
- average return
- net P&L
- sample grade
- evidence level
- reliability
- evidence-qualified historical verdict

The Historical Match score is deliberately moderated by sample reliability.
A tiny sample cannot generate an extreme score simply because one or two trades
were profitable.

The scorecard is descriptive. It does not predict future returns or recommend
live trades.
